Shoulder Pain Treatment in Lighthouse Point

Shoulder discomfort can limit everything from simple daily tasks to sports and work. Whether you're dealing with rotator cuff strain, adhesive capsulitis, tendinopathy, or general shoulder dysfunction, finding the right treatment approach matters. At McNamara Chiropractic Center, we focus on non-surgical shoulder pain relief using targeted therapy and hands-on care designed to restore function and reduce your symptoms.

What We Offer for Shoulder Problems

Our Shoulder Restoration Program combines chiropractic adjustments, spinal decompression therapy, and Class IV laser treatment to address the root causes of shoulder pain rather than just masking symptoms. We also use shockwave therapy and therapeutic massage to release tension and improve mobility. Many shoulder issues stem from neck or upper spine misalignment, which our chiropractor evaluates during your first visit. This thorough approach helps us identify whether your pain originates in the joint itself or from referred tension in supporting structures.

We serve patients throughout Broward and Palm Beach County, including Pompano Beach, Deerfield Beach, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, and surrounding communities.

When Chiropractic Care Makes Sense

Chiropractic shoulder treatment works best for mechanical pain—discomfort linked to joint alignment, muscle tension, or nerve irritation. If you have acute inflammation, recent injury, or ongoing symptoms that don't respond to initial care, we'll tell you honestly whether we can help or if you need another opinion. Some conditions benefit from combining chiropractic care with physical therapy or specialist input, and we don't hesitate to recommend that path if it serves you better.

Treatment Frequency and Your Care Plan

Early treatment phases often involve more frequent visits—typically two to three times per week—to calm inflammation and interrupt the cycle of misalignment. During this relief phase, your body's muscles have a tendency to slip back into old patterns within 24 to 48 hours, so consistent visits help your body retain improvements and build strength. As your condition stabilizes, we transition to a corrective phase with weekly or bi-weekly adjustments, then move into a maintenance schedule of once monthly or less to prevent relapse. Your specific plan depends on your injury type and how your body responds.

Is Neck Adjustment Safe?

Yes—neck adjustments performed by a licensed chiropractor are generally safe for most people. While any medical procedure carries minimal risk, serious complications from neck adjustment are extremely rare. We assess your individual health history and discuss any concerns before beginning treatment, and we use techniques appropriate for your condition.
